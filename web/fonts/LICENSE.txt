Fonts in this folder

  Besley             Copyright the Besley Project Authors (Indestructible Type, Owen Earl).
                     A Clarendon-style serif, used for headlines.
  Schibsted Grotesk  Copyright the Schibsted Grotesk Project Authors (Schibsted Media Group,
                     Bakken & Baeck). A sans serif made for news interfaces, used for text.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits use, copying
and redistribution, including on websites, free of charge. The full license text is at
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

They are variable fonts (weights 400 to 900) taken from the Fontsource distribution
(https://fontsource.org) and served from this site so that visitors' browsers do not contact a
third party for fonts. Each family has a "latin" and a "latin-ext" file; Turkish needs both
(for example the letters ğ, ş, İ are in latin-ext).
